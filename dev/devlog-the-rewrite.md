# We rewrote the garage talk

Hey car folks,

Short version: the people in your garage sound more like people now, the
radio stopped making you jump, and the web version loads faster. Version
0.107.1. Here is what landed.

---

## The words got a tune-up

Some of you told us the writing felt stiff. Too polished, too clever, not
how anybody actually talks in a garage. You were right, so we went through
it line by line.

We started with the lines you see the most, especially in your first
season: the tutorial, the texts from your wife, the little notes that pop up
when you start a job, and the first time you meet Dale and Buck. Close to
90 lines are rewritten so far.

A few before and afters:

- **Your wife, before:** "found a socket in the washing machine. adding it
  to the collection on the windowsill."
- **After:** "I found one of your 10mm sockets in the laundry, I'm going to
  throw it in the pile near the kitchen window"

- **Starting a job, before:** "You opened the box, read the instructions for
  two seconds, and decided you'd figure it out."
- **After:** "No real man reads intructions."

- **The barn find, before:** "The tarp says otherwise."
- **After:** "The chewed up wiring harness says otherwise."

Yes, some of the typos are on purpose. Real texts have typos.

There is a lot more to go, and the rest of the rewrites are already in the
works.

---

## The radio got quieter

The garage radio from the last update was too loud. A song would kick in at
full volume after a few minutes of quiet and scare you half to death.

- **Much quieter.** The radio now sits well under the theme song.
- **Every song fades in and out over 10 seconds.** No more jump scares.
- **Your Music slider still runs it**, even in the middle of a fade.

---

## The Social tab fits your screen

On a laptop the Social tab used to scroll a little because the phone was
taller than the window. The phone now sizes itself to your screen, and
anything that doesn't fit scrolls inside the phone instead of the whole
page.

---

## The web version loads faster

The last update stuffed all the art and music into one giant file, and the
browser version started choking on it. The game now loads the art and songs
as it needs them, so the first load is a lot quicker. Since a song only
downloads when it plays, we also bumped all eight up to better sounding
versions.

See you in the shop.
