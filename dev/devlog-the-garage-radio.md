# The garage got a radio

Hey car folks,

Short version: the shop has music now, and it knows when to be quiet.
Version 0.106.0. Here is what landed.

---

## Eight new songs, low and slow

There are eight new lofi tracks for the hours spent under a hood:

- Dusty Corner
- Garage Dust Groove
- Garage Sunset
- Toolbox Junk Drawer
- Workbench After Midnight
- Workshop Afterglow
- Workshop Nightfall
- Workshop Sunday

They are the kind of thing an old radio on the workbench plays at 1am while
you look for the 10mm socket you just had in your hand.

---

## The theme song stays

The blues shuffle you hear when you open the game is still the theme. It
still plays first, and it still fades out after about a minute so you can
hear yourself think.

Then the radio takes over.

---

## Quiet is part of it

The radio does not play wall to wall. Here is how it works:

- **About 2 minutes** after the theme fades, the first song comes on.
- **4 to 8 minutes of quiet** between every song after that.
- **No repeats** until you have heard all eight, and never the same song
  twice in a row.

Music plays for a while, then it goes away and the garage gets quiet again.
Most of a long night in the shop is quiet.

---

## Your volume, your call

The Music slider in Settings runs the radio too.

- **Turn it down** while a song plays and only the level changes.
- **Turn it all the way off** and the radio goes silent until you want it back.
- **Bring it back up from zero** and the theme plays again, then the radio picks
  up from there.

---

## One heads-up for the web version

All eight songs live inside the game file now, so the download is bigger than
it used to be. Give the first load an extra moment to finish.

See you in the shop.
